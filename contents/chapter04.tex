\chapter{Dataset and Experimental Setup}

\section{NTUH Parkinson's Disease Outpatient Clinic Dataset}
\label{sec:dataset}

This study uses a clinical dataset collected at the National Taiwan University Hospital (NTUH) Parkinson's Disease outpatient clinic in collaboration with Dr.~Lin Jing-Xian (林靜嫻). All recordings were captured as 2D videos using the camera of an ordinary smartphone or tablet, so that the resulting cohort is directly representative of the everyday-device scenario targeted by this thesis.

The data come in two temporal cohorts. The \emph{2020 cohort} consists of recordings of variable length collected starting in 2020. The \emph{2025 cohort} consists of recordings standardized to a fixed length of 30 seconds, collected starting in 2025. Together with the medication status of each subject at recording time, these two cohorts form four sub-groups: 2020/on-medication, 2020/off-medication, 2025/on-medication, and 2025/off-medication.

Throughout this thesis, stage~0 is treated as the \emph{healthy} class and stages~1--4 are merged into the \emph{PD} class. To avoid confounding the underlying motor severity with the effect of levodopa therapy, all principal experiments restrict the analysis to off-medication recordings.

\section{Experimental Roadmap}
\label{sec:roadmap}

Five experiments are conducted in this thesis, each motivated by a specific question about which features and which model family give the best PD-versus-healthy discrimination on the NTUH cohorts. Table~\ref{tab:roadmap} summarizes the roadmap.

\begin{table}[H]
    \centering
    \caption{Experimental roadmap}
    \label{tab:roadmap}
    \small
    \setlength{\tabcolsep}{6pt}
    \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.10\linewidth} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}X}
        \hline
        Exp. & Question and configuration \\
        \hline
        1 & Binary classification using thumb-tip features versus all bilateral joint features, with Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost as classifiers. \\
        2 & On bilateral joint features, combine each of three feature-selection methods (ANOVA, L1-regularized Logistic Regression, XGBoost Importance) with XGBoost for binary classification. \\
        3 & Repeat Exp.~2 but for left-hand-only and right-hand-only feature subsets, in order to expose any inter-hand asymmetry. \\
        4 & Augment bilateral features with joint-wise pairwise correlation features and apply the three feature selection methods, again combined with XGBoost. \\
        5 & Replace the hand-crafted pipeline with an adaptive graph convolutional network (AGCN) that learns joint-wise relationships directly. \\
        \hline
    \end{tabularx}
\end{table}

\section{Experimental Setting}
\label{sec:exp_setting}

\subsection*{Within-Cohort Validation: LOOCV}

Within a single cohort, validation is performed with Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV). For Experiments~2 and~3, feature selection is re-run on the training fold of \emph{every} LOOCV iteration, so that no information from the held-out subject can leak into the selected feature subset. The out-of-fold prediction probability of each subject is stored, and the pooled probabilities are then used to draw a single ROC curve per configuration. After the LOOCV loop is complete, the Youden index
\begin{equation}
    J = \mathrm{TPR}(\tau) - \mathrm{FPR}(\tau)
\end{equation}
is computed over all candidate thresholds, and the threshold $\tau^{*}$ that maximizes $J$ is used to binarize the probabilities into class predictions. Accuracy and F1-score are then computed from the resulting confusion matrix.

\subsection*{Cross-Cohort Evaluation}

In addition to the within-cohort LOOCV protocol, every experiment is also evaluated under a stricter cross-cohort transfer protocol: the model is trained on the entire 2020 off-medication cohort and tested on the entire 2025 off-medication cohort. Feature selection is performed exclusively on the training cohort, and the selected feature indices are then transferred to the unseen test cohort. AUROC, accuracy, and F1-score are reported on the test cohort.

\subsection*{Training-Termination Criteria}

Different model families use different criteria to decide when training has converged.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Logistic Regression} stops when the change in the training loss between consecutive iterations falls below a fixed threshold.
    \item \textbf{Random Forest} and \textbf{XGBoost} are configured to grow up to a fixed number of trees / nodes (see the hyperparameter table in Section~\ref{sec:exp_setting_hparams}).
\end{itemize}
