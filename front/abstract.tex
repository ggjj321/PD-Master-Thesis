\begin{abstract}
\setlength{\parskip}{4pt}
\setstretch{1.3}

本論文以日常智慧型手機或平板電腦錄製之手部旋轉影片，研究帕金森氏症（PD）之二元分類。所提四階段流程為：(i) 透過 MediaPipe Hands 提取每幀雙手各 21 個三維關鍵點；(ii) 對各關節軸計算頻率、清晰度及四種振幅表示（逐週期、滾動峰對峰、滾動均方根、希爾伯特包絡），共形成 1,764 維特徵向量；(iii) 以 ANOVA F 值、L1 正則化邏輯斯迴歸、XGBoost 增益重要性共 3 種方法進行特徵選擇，各取前 10 個特徵，合計 30 次特徵選擇；(iv) 以 XGBoost 或特徵式 AGCN 風格模型作為分類器。實驗於臺大醫院 2020 及 2025 年兩個停藥錄影世代，分別採留一法交叉驗證（LOOCV）及跨世代遷移協定評估。主要發現如下：第一，左手特徵搭配 XGBoost 增益重要性選擇為最強單手基線，前 10 名特徵中左手佔比於 2020 世代為 25:5、2025 世代為 19:11，與運動儲備假說相容。第二，加入關節間配對相關特徵僅帶來微小跨世代 AUROC 提升（$+0.02$--$0.03$），無法穩定改善準確率或 F1 分數。第三，AGCN 於 LOOCV 下 AUROC 約達 $0.98$，但跨世代遷移時降至 $0.59$，低於最佳手工特徵基線（$0.68$），顯示其可學習圖拓撲難以跨越小型臨床資料集中的領域偏移。若將 2020 LOOCV、2025 LOOCV 與 2020-to-2025 跨世代遷移三個設定下的 AUROC、準確率與 F1 分數整體平均，左手特徵結合 XGBoost 重要性選擇達到最高整體平均（$0.69$；平均 AUROC/準確率/F1 為 $0.72/0.76/0.58$），因此為最穩健的手工特徵基線。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}
\setlength{\parskip}{4pt}
\setstretch{1.3}

This thesis investigates binary classification of Parkinson's Disease (PD) from hand-rotation videos recorded with everyday smartphones or tablets. The proposed four-stage pipeline consists of: (i)~3D hand keypoint extraction via MediaPipe Hands, yielding 21 landmark trajectories per hand per frame; (ii)~kinematic feature extraction computing frequency, clarity, and four complementary amplitude representations---cycle-by-cycle, rolling peak-to-peak, rolling RMS, and Hilbert envelope---forming a 1,764-dimensional feature vector; (iii)~feature selection via three methods---ANOVA F-value, L1-regularized logistic regression, and XGBoost gain-based importance---each retaining the top 10 features, for a total of 30 feature selections; and (iv)~binary classification with XGBoost or a feature-based AGCN-style model. Experiments are conducted on two NTUH clinical cohorts (2020 and 2025) of off-medication recordings under Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation (LOOCV) and a 2020-to-2025 cross-cohort transfer protocol. The principal findings are as follows. First, the left-hand feature set with XGBoost gain-based importance selection forms the strongest single-hand baseline; the top-10 selected features favor the left hand by 25:5 in the 2020 cohort and 19:11 in the 2025 cohort, qualitatively compatible with the motor reserve hypothesis. Second, augmenting the bilateral feature set with pairwise joint-correlation features yields only marginal cross-cohort AUROC improvement ($+0.02$--$0.03$) without a stable gain in accuracy or F1-score. Third, AGCN achieves AUROC of approximately 0.98 under LOOCV but collapses to 0.59 in cross-cohort transfer, falling below the best hand-crafted feature baseline (0.68), indicating that its learned graph topology does not generalize across recording protocols in small clinical datasets. When robustness is defined as the average of AUROC, accuracy, and F1-score across 2020 LOOCV, 2025 LOOCV, and 2020-to-2025 cross-cohort transfer, left-hand features with XGBoost gain-based importance selection achieve the highest overall hand-crafted baseline average (0.69; mean AUROC/accuracy/F1-score = 0.72/0.76/0.58).

\end{abstract*}
